DEPARTMENT: National Database & Registration Authority (NADRA)
SERVICE: CNIC Renewal & Smart Card Issuance

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Pakistani citizen holding an expired or expiring CNIC / Smart Card.
- Minimum age 18 years.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR RENEWAL:
1. Original expired CNIC or CNIC copy.
2. Photocopy of Father's or Spouse's CNIC.
3. Proof of address (Utility bill or Domicile if address is changed).
4. Biometric verification at NADRA e-Sahulat or counter.

FEE STRUCTURE & PROCESSING TIME:
- Normal Processing: PKR 750 (Processing time: 15 to 30 working days)
- Urgent Processing: PKR 1500 (Processing time: 7 to 10 working days)
- Executive Processing: PKR 2500 (Processing time: 2 to 5 working days)

OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://id.nadra.gov.pk
POLICY NOTES: Standard CNIC renewal requires valid identity verification and updated address details.
