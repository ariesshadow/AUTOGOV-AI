DEPARTMENT: Directorate General of Immigration & Passports (DGIP)
SERVICE: Machine Readable Passport (MRP) / E-Passport Renewal

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Pakistani citizen holding an expired or expiring Passport.
- Valid CNIC or NICOP required for applicants aged 18 and above.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR RENEWAL:
1. Original CNIC / Smart Card / NICOP with 1 photocopy.
2. Existing Original Passport with 1 photocopy of the first two pages.
3. Bank Fee Payment Challan or e-Payment digital receipt.
4. No Objection Certificate (NOC) for government employees.

FEE STRUCTURE & PROCESSING TIME (5-Year / 36-Page Passport):
- Normal Processing: PKR 4,500 (Processing time: 15 to 21 working days)
- Urgent Processing: PKR 7,500 (Processing time: 5 working days)
- Fast-Track Processing: PKR 12,500 (Processing time: 2 working days)

FEE STRUCTURE & PROCESSING TIME (10-Year / 36-Page Passport):
- Normal Processing: PKR 6,700 (Processing time: 15 to 21 working days)
- Urgent Processing: PKR 11,200 (Processing time: 5 working days)

OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk
POLICY NOTES: Physical appearance is required at the nearest Passport Office or Embassy for biometric and photograph capture unless applying via the online portal for renewal without photo changes.
